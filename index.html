Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

StreakAnywhere ("the Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Extension collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

The extension stores only the information required to provide its functionality.

  • Tracked websites selected by you.
  • Daily streak history.
  • Heatmap activity.
  • Achievements and statistics.
  • User preferences and settings.

The extension does not collect browsing history except for determining whether the current tab matches one of the websites you have chosen to track.

Permissions

Storage

Used to save your streaks, tracked websites, preferences, achievements, heatmap data, and extension settings.

Tabs

Used only to detect whether the active tab belongs to one of your tracked websites so your streak can be updated.

Notifications

Used to remind you before a streak expires and to notify you when milestones or achievements are reached.

Alarms

Used to schedule periodic background tasks such as updating streaks and sending reminders.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally using Chrome Storage. If Chrome Sync is enabled, your settings and streak data may be synchronized between your own Chrome browsers using Google's Chrome Sync service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Remote Code

The extension does not execute remotely hosted JavaScript or WebAssembly. All executable code is packaged with the extension.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your stored data. Only the minimum permissions required for the extension's functionality are requested.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
